Arabic meeting notes, written on your own computer

Modwen is free, open-source meeting software built for Arabic first. It records your meeting, transcribes Arabic and English, even in the same sentence, and writes the summary, decisions and action items. Nothing is sent to the cloud.

Why most meeting tools struggle with Arabic

Real meetings in the Arab world rarely stay in one language. A sentence starts in Arabic and ends in English, product names and technical terms stay in English, and people speak their own dialect. Most transcription tools pick one language per recording and either translate the English away or turn it into Arabic letters.

«خلاص اتفقنا، بكرة تسليم الـ MVP، و Lina بتخلص الـ QA قبل الأربعاء.»

Modwen decides the language sentence by sentence and keeps each word in the script it was spoken in, so this line stays exactly like this in the transcript.

What you get after the meeting

  • A transcript with who said what, in Arabic, English or both. Gulf, Levantine, Egyptian Arabic and MSA.
  • A summary in English, Arabic or both, your choice per meeting.
  • Decisions, action items with owners and real deadlines, and open questions. "بكرة", "الخميس الجاي" or "by end of day" become dates in your time zone.
  • Every point links to the moment it was said. Click it and the recording plays from there.
  • Speakers named by voice in later meetings, once you've named them (with their consent).

Private by design

The recording, the transcription and the AI model that writes your notes all run on your computer. Modwen downloads its models once and then works offline. It refuses, in code, to talk to any AI server that isn't on your own machine. Recordings are encrypted with a separate key per meeting.

That matters for meetings you can't upload: client calls, HR conversations, legal and government work.

Works with the meetings you already have

Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom or a meeting in the room: Modwen records your microphone and the meeting audio from your computer. Live captions show while you talk, and a notification tells you when the notes are ready.

Questions

Does Modwen transcribe Arabic dialects?

Yes. It uses Whisper large-v3, which handles Modern Standard Arabic well and Gulf, Levantine and Egyptian Arabic increasingly well, including English words inside Arabic sentences.

Can it write the meeting summary in Arabic?

Yes, in English, Arabic or both. The whole app is also available in Arabic, with a right-to-left layout.

What computer do I need?

16 GB of memory at least. Best: an Apple Silicon Mac with 32 GB, or a Windows PC with an NVIDIA graphics card (12 GB or more). Modwen checks your computer and picks models that fit.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under Apache-2.0. No account, no subscription, no usage limits. See what's included.
