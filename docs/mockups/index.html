Let participants know they're being recorded.

Heads-up: I'm using Modwen to record and transcribe this meeting for notes. The audio stays on my machine. Tell me if you'd prefer I stop.
تنبيه: أستخدم أداة Modwen لتسجيل هذا الاجتماع وتفريغه لكتابة المحضر. التسجيل يبقى على جهازي فقط. أخبروني إذا كنتم تفضلون إيقافه.

  • Language: Auto for mixed meetings. Use English only or Arabic only when you know the meeting is in one language; it's slightly more accurate and faster.
  • There is no cloud option. The local-only status is a label, not a toggle.

  • Evidence links: every note item cites the transcript lines it came from. Clicking one highlights the lines and plays the audio from there; clicking a transcript line highlights the notes that cite it.
