Private by design

Everything happens inside your machine.

Recording, transcription, speaker detection and the AI that writes your notes all run on your own computer. Modwen refuses to talk to any server that isn't localhost. That rule is in the code, not in a policy.

✓No cloud, no account, no telemetry. Nothing to sign up for, nothing phoning home.
✓Encrypted recordings. A separate AES-256 key for every meeting; delete the meeting and the key is gone.
✓Consent built in. A ready-made notice in Arabic and English, every time you press Start.
✓Even this page loads no fonts, trackers or scripts from anywhere else.

Get started

Download

Grab the latest release zip (about 0.5 MB) and unzip it anywhere.

Double-click

Modwen.command on macOS, Modwen.bat on Windows. The setup wizard picks models for your hardware and downloads them once.

Talk

Press Start in your next meeting. Press Stop. Read your notes.

Questions

Does anything really stay offline?

Yes. After the one-time model downloads, Modwen works with your internet turned off. AI endpoints must be localhost; anything else is refused in code.

What computer do I need?

16 GB of RAM minimum. Best: an Apple Silicon Mac with 32 GB, or a Windows PC with an NVIDIA GPU (12 GB+). Modwen detects your hardware and picks models that fit.

How good is the Arabic?

Transcription uses Whisper large-v3, which handles MSA well and Gulf, Levantine and Egyptian dialects increasingly well, including English words inside Arabic sentences. Summaries are best with Qwen3-8B or larger.

Can I use my own model?

Yes: any OpenAI-compatible server on your machine (llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM). Switching is one setting.

Is it free?

Free and open source under Apache-2.0. No accounts, no subscriptions, no usage limits.
