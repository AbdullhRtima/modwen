Looking for a Meetily alternative?

Meetily and Modwen are both open-source meeting note takers that run on your own computer. The differences: in Modwen everything is free, including speaker identification, and it's built for Arabic and English spoken together.

Where Meetily is ahead

Meetily is older and much more widely used, with a large community. It has meeting templates and a fast Parakeet engine for European languages today. If your meetings are only in English or another European language and you're happy with the free tier, it's a solid choice.

Where Modwen is different

  • Arabic first. Gulf, Levantine, Egyptian Arabic and MSA, with English terms kept in English inside Arabic sentences, summaries in Arabic or English, and a full right-to-left interface. More on Arabic meeting notes.
  • Nothing behind a paywall. Speaker identification, voice profiles and your own model are free. See what's included.
  • Local only, enforced. Modwen won't talk to any AI server that isn't on your computer. How the offline pipeline works.
